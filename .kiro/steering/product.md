# Product Overview

App Suite is a comprehensive business management platform that combines AI-powered tools with traditional business operations. The platform serves as both a client-facing website and an internal admin dashboard for managing business operations.

## Core Features

- **AI-Powered Tools**: Chat interfaces, image generation, proposal generation, and content creation
- **Business Management**: Client management, project tracking, task management, and sales pipeline
- **Content Platform**: Blog, whitepapers, documentation, and resource center
- **Admin Dashboard**: Comprehensive admin interface for managing all business operations
- **Authentication**: Supabase-based authentication with role-based access control

## Target Users

- **External Users**: Potential clients browsing services, reading content, and requesting proposals
- **Internal Users**: Admin users managing clients, projects, tasks, and business operations
- **Content Consumers**: Visitors accessing whitepapers, blog posts, and educational resources

## Key Business Areas

- SEO and digital marketing services
- AI development and integration
- Business automation solutions
- Technology consulting and partnerships
